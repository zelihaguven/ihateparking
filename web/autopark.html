HONEST LIMITATION

Empty bays count as drivable space, so routes may cut across them; a real lot needs lane and direction rules. The lot is static and the parked cars are ground truth, not detections. On every free bay of five 50%-occupied lots the planner parked 54 of 54 with no collisions and at most 11.2 cm / 2.1° final error. That is simulation evidence only.
